How Inquiries Work

How do I send an inquiry?

+
Browse products and click "Send Inquiry". Your inquiry goes directly to the seller via WhatsApp.

Do I have to pay through Klean Loom?

+
No. Payments and negotiations happen directly with the seller.

Can I contact multiple sellers for the same product?

+
Yes, you can contact multiple boutiques for the same product.

Is my personal info shared with sellers?

+
Only necessary details are shared. Your data is never sold.
