FAQ: How Inquiries Work

How do I send an inquiry?

+
Browse products and click "Send Inquiry". Your inquiry will go to the seller via WhatsApp.

Do I have to pay through Klean Loom?

+
No. All payments and negotiations are directly handled between you and the seller.

Can I contact multiple sellers for the same product?

+
Yes, you can send inquiries to multiple sellers if the product is listed by more than one boutique.

Is my personal info shared with sellers?

+
Only the details necessary to process your inquiry are shared. We do not sell your data.
