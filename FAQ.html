FAQ: How Inquiries Work

Our platform allows users to send inquiries directly to sellers. Here's the process:

  • Browse products listed by various boutiques.
  • Click on "Send Inquiry" for any product you like.
  • Your inquiry goes directly to the seller via WhatsApp (no payment processed by us).
  • Sellers will respond to confirm availability, price, or any other details.
  • All negotiations, payment, and delivery are handled directly between you and the seller.

Klean Loom ensures inquiries reach sellers safely and promptly.
