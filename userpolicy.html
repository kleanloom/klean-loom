User Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At Klean Loom, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. By using our platform, you agree to the practices described below.

We collect the following information when you use our service:

  • Name

  • User preferences, inquiries, and feedback

We do not collect sensitive personal data unless voluntarily provided by the user.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to inquiries or service requests

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • Your data is stored securely on our servers.
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your information.
  • Only authorized personnel have access to user data.

4. Sharing of Information

  • Your contact details are shared with a seller only when you initiate an inquiry.

We never share your data for advertising or external marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Services & Cookies

  • We may use analytics tools and cookies to improve website experience.
  • Cookies do not contain personally identifiable information.
  • Users can opt out of cookies via browser settings.
  • Our platform does not automatically share your data with third-party apps.

6. User Rights

  • Access your stored data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Opt out of promotional notifications

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Continued use of the platform after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
