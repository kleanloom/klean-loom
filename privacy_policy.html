Seller Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At Klean Loom, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information from sellers on our platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name, business/boutique name
  • Contact information (WhatsApp number, email)
  • Google authentication info (name & email via OAuth)
  • Product listings, images, and descriptions
  • Business location and category

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage seller accounts
  • Verify seller identity and shop details
  • Provide seller dashboard features
  • Forward product inquiries from users
  • Notify sellers about important updates or platform changes
  • Improve seller tools and enhance platform performance

3. Data Security

  • Product listing information is visible to platform users
  • We implement industry-standard security measures to protect seller information
  • All communications through Klean Loom are securely transmitted
  • Seller identity is protected until a user initiates an inquiry

4. Cookies & Analytics

  • We may use cookies or analytics tools to improve website experience, analyze trends, and optimize platform performance
  • No personally identifiable information is shared with advertisers

5. Google Authentication

  • Google OAuth is used for seller login
  • We only receive your basic profile information (name & email)
  • We do not access your Google password or sensitive data

6. Your Rights

  • Access your stored data
  • Update or correct your information
  • Request deletion of your account

7. Changes to This Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy at any time
  • Changes take effect immediately when published on our website
